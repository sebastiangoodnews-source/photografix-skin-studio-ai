INSTALL (about two minutes)
================================================================

1. Install "Adobe UXP Developer Tool" (free) from the Adobe Creative Cloud
   desktop app, and make sure Photoshop 24.2 or newer is installed.

2. Open Photoshop first, then open UXP Developer Tool.

3. In UXP Developer Tool click "Add Plugin…".

4. Select this file from the unpacked download:

       plugin/photografix-skin-studio/manifest.json

5. Click "Load".

6. In Photoshop: Window > Extensions (or Plugins) > Photografix Skin Studio AI.
   The panel opens docked on the right.

The plugin works offline and never contacts a server. No account, no key,
no credits, no subscription.

================================================================
FIRST RUN
================================================================

1. Open a portrait photograph.
2. In the panel press "Analyse" so the plugin reads the face and body.
3. Work down the seven stages. "Preview" shows a stage without committing it;
   "Apply stage" commits it. "Build Photoshop layers" writes the whole stack
   into your document as editable layer groups.
4. Nothing is ever flattened. Your original layer stays untouched at the bottom
   of the stack, and every Photografix stage is its own group you can hide,
   reorder or delete.

================================================================
WHAT TO TRY FIRST
================================================================

For a normal portrait retouch, the defaults are deliberately conservative:

  Stage 1  Frequency Separation  -> Normal mode, radius 6-10
  Stage 2  Micro Dodge & Burn    -> Very subtle
  Stage 3  Sculpt Dodge & Burn   -> Natural
  Stage 4  African Skin Tone     -> Auto-match on
  Stage 5  Colour Grading        -> your look
  Stage 6  Glossy Skin           -> Subtle glow
  Stage 7  Final Review          -> read the checks, change nothing

Stage 7 measures texture loss, skin-lightness drift, edge halos, clipping and
face-to-body colour matching against your original, and tells you what it
found. If it says PASS, you are done.

================================================================
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
================================================================

"Add Plugin" refuses the file
    UXP Developer Tool needs manifest.json at the root of the folder you
    select. If you unpacked twice you may have a folder inside a folder;
    select the inner one.

The panel opens but says "Preview mode"
    Photoshop was not running, or the plugin was opened in a browser. Launch
    Photoshop and load the plugin again from UXP Developer Tool.

"Build Photoshop layers" does nothing
    It only works with a real document open in Photoshop. On a wrong document
    (32-bit, or a document with no pixel layer) it reports why in the panel.
